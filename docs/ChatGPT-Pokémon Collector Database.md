## Features that would make ArchiveDex stand out

I think these would be the signature features that no other archive currently combines:

1. **Advanced recommendation engine** based on filters rather than popularity.
2. **Per-category ratings** plus a separate overall score.
3. **Side-by-side comparison** of games.
4. **Interactive custom region map** linking games by geography.
5. **Interactive development timeline** showing the evolution of Pokémon fan projects.
6. **Rich game documentation** with screenshots, changelogs, and feature badges instead of short descriptions.
7. **Creator profiles** connecting all works by the same developer.
8. **Powerful tag system** with hundreds of searchable tags.
9. **Curated collections** rather than simple top-10 lists.
10. **Preservation focus**, distinguishing complete, active, historical, and discontinued projects while linking users to official sources where possible.
